Amber Rewards in the bag: final prototypes

Each shopper has its own row, with Option 1 and Option 2 side by side, and every phone works. Option 1 keeps the section always open in the bag. Option 2 keeps it closed, showing only the key information, and opens the section in a modal. To link, enter the code 123456.

Names, emails, balances and prices are sample data, at a rate of 100 points to AED 1. Any other code shows the wrong-code state, and three wrong tries lock it for 30 seconds.
